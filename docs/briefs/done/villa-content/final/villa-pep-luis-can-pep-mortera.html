Villa Pep Luis (Can Pep Mortera) is a beautifully renovated Ibiza farmhouse in peaceful countryside just a few minutes from the airport and the main roads into Ibiza Town — one of our most popular family villas year after year. The owners' stylish renovation blends the original finca with modern comfort: three bedrooms, every one with its own en-suite shower room, plus an extended pool and a gorgeous outside space.

Inside the villa

An electric gate opens onto a driveway with space for two cars. The farmhouse's typically thick stone walls keep the living area wonderfully cool in high summer, and the stylish renovated kitchen leads off it. The three bedrooms each have their own en-suite shower room and are finished in the same warm mix of original finca character and modern comfort.

The pool and outside space

The extended pool is the villa's showpiece — spacious and private, with plenty of room for lounging in sun or shade, a wonderful shaded dining area and a barbecue. The renovation took this from a simple, charming villa to something really rather special.
