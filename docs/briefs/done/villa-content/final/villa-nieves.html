Villa Nieves is a rustic Ibiza farmhouse built for big groups — six bedrooms and four bathrooms, sleeping up to eleven, across the air-conditioned main house and a separate annex, with high ceilings, extensive grounds and a huge pool. It sits in quiet countryside a five-minute drive from San Rafael, with the main routes to Ibiza Town, Playa d'en Bossa and San Antonio all close at hand: real peace and privacy, without ever being far from the action.

Inside the villa

Through the original stone archway, the annex sits on your left — simply furnished, air-conditioned, with a shaded terrace to the front. The main farmhouse is entered through a small walled garden terrace with a large brick-built barbecue; inside, beautifully preserved heavy finca doors open from the big kitchen-diner onto the garden, and the open-plan living area beyond stays cool through high summer.

  • Annex — two doubles, a twin and a small bathroom
  • Main house, ground floor — one double with en-suite
  • Main house, first floor — two further bedrooms and the roof terrace

The pool and outside space

The outside spaces are what guests love most here: gardens with palm trees, flowering bushes and lawns, a huge, inviting pool, and plenty of sun loungers and shaded tables for a poolside drink between dips. The roof terrace looks out over the pool and across open countryside.
