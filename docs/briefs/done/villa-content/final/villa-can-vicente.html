Villa Can Vincente is a large villa near San Antonio — peaceful and private, yet just a few minutes' taxi ride from the nightlife of San Antonio and the bars and beach clubs of the Bay. Seven air-conditioned bedrooms make it one of our biggest houses, long popular with groups who want space, a big pool, and a night out within easy reach of home.

Inside the villa

A large sliding gate opens onto a huge driveway with plenty of parking. The entrance leads into a central hallway connecting everything.

  • Ground floor — three twin bedrooms, a large modern kitchen-diner, and two shower rooms (one newly fitted)
  • First floor — three more twins, and a large bedroom with a double and a single bed and patio doors onto a sun terrace, plus a newly fitted bathroom and a further shower room

The pool and outside space

The wonderful large pool has plenty of room to spread out the sun loungers, looking across the countryside towards San Antonio Bay. A shaded terrace holds the outside dining area and a brick-built barbecue — a great spot for long, lazy lunches out of the intense high-summer sun.
