Villa Torres is a modern eight-bedroom villa — six doubles and two twins, fully air-conditioned — within a ten-minute walk of central Playa d'en Bossa. It's rare to find a villa this size at such good value so close to the resort: you can see the Ushuaïa tower from one of the upstairs bedrooms, the village of San Jordi with its shops, banks, bars and supermarkets is a seven-minute walk the other way, and Ibiza Town is a few minutes by taxi. An ideal choice for big groups who don't want to hire a car, with a completely open communal living space — the perfect spot to catch up on the previous day's exploits.

Inside the villa

The villa is finished throughout in a clean, modern style to a good standard, with the bedrooms split across its two floors and all the bathrooms fitted as showers.

  • Ground floor — an open-plan lounge and kitchen-diner, one twin bedroom and two doubles
  • First floor — four en-suite doubles

The pool and outside space

A lovely pool area with a shaded space for outside dining — the spot to regroup after a night in the resort. There is a brick-built barbecue and plenty of sun beds to enjoy the sun.
