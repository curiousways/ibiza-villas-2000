Villa KM2 — also known as Villa Maria — does what its name says: it sits just two kilometres outside Ibiza Town, with Playa d'en Bossa also a five-minute drive away. Stylish, redecorated and air-conditioned throughout, it's a private, genuinely convenient base for families and mixed groups: five bedrooms — four doubles and a twin — sleeping ten, a lovely big pool, and a proper outdoor kitchen and dining area.

Inside the villa

An electric gate opens onto plenty of parking; the entrance at the side of the house leads into a stylish hallway. To the left is a large living room with a TV, music system and comfortable sofas, with a well-equipped modern kitchen next door.

  • Ground floor — two good-sized double bedrooms and a stylish, modern bathroom with shower
  • First floor — two doubles, a twin, and another bathroom with shower

The pool and outside space

The grounds are one of the big attractions of this house: a lovely, large pool with an outside shower room and toilet, a terrace with plenty of room for sunbathing on loungers, and an outside kitchen and dining area — ideal for long lunches by the pool or evening meals under the stars.
