The West coast of Ibiza is famed for its incredible sunsets and the wonderful choice of small to medium size sandy coves, known as 'Calas.' Starting from just north of San Antonio and travelling southwards, these are some of our favourite sunbathing spots you can take your pick of. Cala Salada This popular beach has beautiful turquoise water, a great chiringuito for snacks and drinks and a wonderful restaurant to the left that serves wonderful seafood and paella. It can get pretty crowded on the sand in the height of summer so you either need to get there early or, if you don't mind a short scramble over a fairly rocky coastal path, you can reach the second part of the beach to your right, known locally as Cala Saladeta. At the beginning of the road that leads down to Cala Salada is another great little restaurant called Can Pau, that is a bit more tranquil, so you could always stop off for a bite to eat then head down to the beach from there. Cala Gracio and Cala Gracioneta Heading south, just before you reach the main town of San Antonio is the lovely cove of Cala Gracio. The powdery sand reaches back a fair distance meaning you can normally find a spot to sunbathe even in the peak summer months. There is a small, family run restaurant on the beach serving typical Spanish style food and local wines and beers. To the right of the sand is a path across the rocks that is fairly easy to navigate and brings you to the little beach of Cala Gracioneta. Space is limited in this tiny cove, but it is home to one of our favourite restaurants on the island, El Chiringuito. Owned by the family who also have Mambo, Savannah and Bondi in San Antonio, this pretty beachfront restaurant is a really special location and is a popular choice for couples getting married on the island, so make sure you reserve a table if you fancy a long, leisurely lunch with your toes in the sand. S'Arenal beach and Es Pueto S'Arenal is the central beach of San Antonio, and hugs the shoreline of the Bay, flanked by the 'paseo' which is the popular promenade along the coast. This beach is a perennial favourite with seasonal workers and young holidaymakers due to its lively vibe and its proximity to the rows of bars and restaurants including Linekers, the Ibiza Rocks bar, Itaca and The Bay Bar. Not the most peaceful of beaches, this is a great place to come if you want to socialise and meet new faces - as long as you don't mind endless interruptions from numerous PR's perching next to your towel trying to sell you tickets to clubs and boat parties! If you follow the paseo a few minutes walk around the headland you come to the rather more peaceful Es Pueto, which is a pretty little beach with fabulous shallow, clear water. There is a little jetty where you can rent 'Starboats' which are small four-man motorboats that don't need a licence. There is a wonderful chiringuito in the middle of the beach serving simple Spanish food and fresh seafood at super cheap prices. There is also a row of restaurants behind the beach including one serving excellent home made pizzas and huge salads. Playa Pinet Continuing along the west coast brings you to Playa Pinet which is a beautiful, circular bay with a broad beach and a choice of three fantastic beach bars. The VIP culture hasn't permeated Playa Pinet yet, so the options for eating and drinking here are still reasonably priced and good value. There is a little jetty to the right of the beach where a boat goes back and forth from San Antonio Port every half an hour. Port des Torrent Port des Torrent is a gorgeous bay that has grown in popularity over the last few years, partly due to the Imagination beach bar. This wonderful, rustic venue is like a little west coast beach club but with great value food and drinks and a distinctly 'hippy' vibe. There is chill-out style shaded seating around low tables on the beach plus further benches and tables on a shaded terrace to the side.  At sunset, the owners rearrange the tables and chairs for sunset so everyone can enjoy a cocktail looking out over the sea to watch the sun go down. And we reckon the Mojitos at Imagination are the tastiest and best value around! Port des Torrent also has a jetty served by the local water taxis so you can get around to the surrounding calas and across to San Antonio. Cala Bassa Cala Bassa is a simply stunning bay that has become one of the 'glossiest' beaches of the West coast over the last few years. Many A list celebrities have been photographed here and it is a fun and lively 'see and be seen' type of location. But be warned, if you're planning on coming here in July and August, you'd be as well to book yourself a day bed at the enormous Cala Bassa beach club that now stretches the full length of the beach in high season. It's been a bit of a bone of contention amongst locals and residents over the last couple of years, since the edges of the road leading to Cala Bassa have been blocked with boulders, preventing anyone parking there. All visitors must now park in the Cala Bassa Beach club car park and pay the daily charge, even if just visiting for a few hours. However, the beach club is gorgeous and the food is excellent. It's not cheap, but you wouldn't expect it to be in such a prime location with the most incredible view. If you fancy a visit to this stunning bay then our advice is to book a bed, take a taxi, plenty of cash and set yourself up for a day of beachfront luxury. Cala Comte is famed for its turquoise water lapping the beach that wraps round the rocky coastline. There are two main sandy areas plus a little beach to the left called Cala Contita that has a fantastic chiringuito on the water's edge. In the middle of the headland is the famous Sunset Ashram, a stunning circular bar and restaurant. As the name suggests, this is a fabulous vantage point for sunset watching while enjoying delicious cocktails or an evening meal from their varied international fusion menu. Further to the right of the main beach are two more restaurants, Silla des Bosc and Ses Roques, both serving wonderful food with the same incredible views out to sea.   Cala Tarida Cala Vedella Cala Carbo Cala d'Hort
