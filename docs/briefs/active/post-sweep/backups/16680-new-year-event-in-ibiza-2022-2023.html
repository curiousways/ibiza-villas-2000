New Year Event in Ibiza 2022/2023

With this New Year Event in Ibiza celebrations just around the corner, it looks like Ibiza is heading out of 2022 as only the White Isle can. With many of the big island names throwing their hats into the NYE party hat this year, check out our guide to some of the best ways to see 2023 in with true Ibiza style. Pacha  31st December Music On (Marco Carola) After what has been a mammoth year for Pacha it’s great to see the return of their legendary NYEs parties. And who better to see in the coming year than Marco Carola and Music on? Tickets are going fast, click HERE to get yours now!   DC10 1st jan Circo Loco (Archie Hamilton, Cassy and Tania Vulcano) The absolute island favourite has to be the New Years' day celebration at DC10. Many locals forgo the usual shenanigans on the 31st to arrive fresh at 6 pm on the 1st to kick off New Year as only Circo Loco knows how. Of course, there is always the option to head over after one of the other event in Ibiza but the question is will you make it through to 6 am on the 2nd? Tickets are available HERE   Amnesia 30th December Pyramid (Lineup TBA) We are still waiting for lineups but at just 25 euros a ticket, Amnesia might be the perfect place to start your NYE celebrations. Pyramid was one of the stars of the summer season this year, with packed dancefloors most nights, don’t miss out on tickets, get yours HERE   Amnesia 1st Jan Elrow (Lineup TBC) The mighty Elrow crashes into the NYE offerings with their usual bang. Going up against DC10 is a bit ask but with a surge of interest for New Years' eve celebrations on the island its great to see a fun alternative to keep that party going for as long as possible HERE   Lio 31st December New Year's Eve at Lio (Jon Rocca) NYE at Lios is now almost an island institution, enjoy the cabaret and show whilst the clock strikes 12 and then enjoy the club until late. It’s definitely a pricey option but it is the best place to see some of the island's midnight fireworks celebrations.
