Where is San Antonio?

San Antonio, located on Ibiza's west coast, is one of the island's most sought-after tourist hotspots, attracting like a powerful magnet, year after year, hordes of young revellers from the UK and other parts of Europe. San Antonio's appeal can be attributed to many factors, the main one being its breathtaking location. The city is nestled in San Antonio Bay, renowned as one of the world's most stunning natural harbours. What to do in San Antonio?

San Antonio, renowned for its extensive assortment of bars, clubs, and restaurants, caters to both locals and tourists alike. The heart of the city is home to super-clubs Eden and Es Paradis, while other popular clubs like Amnesia, Privilege, and Underground are about a 15-minute taxi ride away. If pool parties are your preference, venues like O Beach Ibiza and Ibiza Rocks offer unforgettable open-air experiences. Daytime clubbers can head to Ibiza Rocks Hotel or O Beach Ibiza Beach Club to dance under the sun to top DJ performances. The disco bus, running every 30 minutes from midnight, offers another affordable transport option.

The West End

Discover the pulsating heart of Ibiza's nightlife: The West End. This vibrant district, also known as The Strip, is the ultimate entertainment hub, offering an affordable playground for clubbing, revelry, and unforgettable nightlife experiences. Welcome to the renowned Ibiza West End, your go-to destination for an incredible time. Along Carrer Santa Agnès, you'll find pedestrianized streets adorned with various establishments catering to all types of party enthusiasts, from casual bar-goers to dance floor divas. The West End boasts a diverse collection of bars, pubs, and intimate discos, forming a captivating long bar strip. The party kicks off from May until the end of September each year. For the enthusiastic souls, a few bars keep the spirit alive during April and October, ensuring the excitement never fades. However, during the quieter months from November to March, most of the West End's bars hibernate, eagerly awaiting the return of lively crowds. Look no further than the West End. Experience a world of entertainment, delicious food and good company amid the pulsating energy of this fantastic downtown hot spot.

The Sunset Strip

San Antonio is renowned for its iconic Sunset Strip, a paradise for party animals seeking a diverse selection of bars. This vibrant area draws large crowds every evening, all eager to witness the breathtaking sunset while grooving to the mesmerizing beats spun by world-famous DJs at legendary spots like Café del Mar and Café Mambo. If you're looking for an unforgettable sunset experience combined with top-notch music, the Sunset Strip is the ultimate go-to destination.

Other Activities

San Antonio offers more than just bars, clubs, and soul-refreshing parties. Visitors can indulge in a plethora of water sports and leisure activities, all while immersing themselves in the breathtaking natural surroundings of the area.

Eden

Eden, in line with its club philosophy, stands as a prominent player in the electronic scene, proudly holding the title of San Antonio's second super-club. Under new management, the venue underwent a complete architectural, aesthetic, and musical transformation in 2013.

Es Paradis

Nestled atop one of the world's most stunning islands, this club presents the epitome of holiday clubbing experience. Its crown jewel is the renowned Fiesta Del Agua, an extraordinary water party that remains an exclusive part of the club's cherished traditions. Prepare for an unparalleled and unforgettable experience at this breathtaking venue.

Benimussa Park

Benimussa Park is n unique on the island. What was once a former zoo in San Antonio has undergone a magnificent transformation into a grand outdoor party club. Founded in 1975, the park now hosts weekly summer parties, as well as a variety of special projects and events, making it a must for party-goers looking for an unforgettable experience.

Ibiza Rocks

Ibiza Rocks offers a seamless blend of exceptional accommodation and the most unforgettable, party-fueled clubbing experiences of your lifetime. Renowned for its exquisite pool parties and upscale dining options, the Ibiza Rocks Hotel is strategically situated by Ibiza Rocks, right at the heart of San Antoni de Portmany. Prepare to immerse yourself in the ultimate Ibiza experience at this vibrant destination.

Café Mambo

Café Mambo, located in Sant Antoni de Portmany on the beautiful island of Ibiza, is famous for winning the DJ Award for Best Bar in Ibiza. It's the ideal place to have fun before a night out in Ibiza's best superclubs. Enjoy a lively pre-party at Café Mambo before plunging into the bewitching nightlife of the island's most prestigious clubs.

O Beach Ibiza

Situated on the West Coast of Ibiza, in San Antonio, O Beach Ibiza is a luxurious beach resort renowned for its wildly entertaining poolside parties and live shows hosted by top-notch DJs. It also boasts captivating aerial performances. The resort's prime location along the majestic S’Arenal waterfront makes it a quintessential and glamorous beach club that epitomizes the perfect seaside destination.

How to get to San Antonio?

San Antonio emphasizes the utmost convenience for its visitors and provides an excellent bus network connecting all major resorts in the area. This efficient system also offers easy access to the airport, which is approximately 20km away. For those seeking a vibrant nightlife experience, San Antonio's renowned disco bus operates throughout the night, shuttling both locals and tourists to various clubs in the region, including Ibiza Town and the clubbing hotspot of Playa d’en Bossa. Moreover, there are parking areas and public car parks available, with options for both free and paid parking. Additionally, finding taxi ranks within the town is hassle-free. Taxi fares in San Antonio and Ibiza in general are reasonable and affordable, especially when traveling in groups.
