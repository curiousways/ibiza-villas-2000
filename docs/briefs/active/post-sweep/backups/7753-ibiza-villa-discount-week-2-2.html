Casa Caleta

Historic

Casa Caleta is in one of the most beautiful and historic locations in Ibiza, just a 300m stroll from Sa Caleta beach, or Es Bol Nou as it's sometimes known. This is the spot that the first ever inhabitants of Ibiza, the Phonecians, settled and made their homes around 800BC. And there's a reason for that! The dramatic red cliffs, crystal clear sea and sheer naked beauty of this area are renowned.
