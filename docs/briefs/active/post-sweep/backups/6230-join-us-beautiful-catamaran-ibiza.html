At Ibiza Villas 2000, we are always looking for wonderful new experiences for our clients, so we are delighted to be able to offer boat trips in Ibiza on this wonderful Catamaran. Nothing beats seeing the stunning Ibiza coastline by boat; it's a unique experience that we encourage everyone to try during their visit to the island. Last week we were lucky enough to spend an afternoon enjoying it for ourselves - all in the name of research of course! We joined the crew down at the Marina Botafoc harbour ready to set sail for a hangover-friendly launch time of 1.30pm. There was quite a crowd of us ready to board, but there is masses of room on this fabulous vessel. In fact, this Catamaran can take groups of up to 60 guests. We set sail with about 45 people on board, which meant there was plenty of room for moving about the boat, changing sunbathing location according to the position of the sun, and even throwing a few dance moves when the tunes ramped up! The boat set off across to Playa den Bossa then headed towards Salines. Before too long, it was time for a little swim-stop as the anchor was lowered, allowing us a refreshing dip in the turquoise water. Next up was the leg of the journey across to the waters opposite Atlantis. Then we changes course to sail in between the incredible rock of Es Vedra and its little neighbouring island of Es Vedranell and headed across to Formentera. It was an amazing way to really get a feel for sailing on such a beautiful vessel. The itinerary offered on this spectacular seven hour trip starts from Marina Botafoc with a welcome drink as your board the Catamaran. Due to overwhelming demand from clients to spend as much time as possible at Formentera, the boat sets sail directly to Ibiza's little sister island where it moors up in the crystal clear turquoise waters of Ses Illetes. This stunning beach was recently voted 'The best beach in Europe' by TripAdvisor. You can read more about it here. The water really has to be seen to be believed; you could easily imagine you were on a Caribbean island. The crew on the Catamaran bring kayaks and Stand Up Paddle boards   There is something really special about seeing Ibiza from the water; it seems to add a whole new perspective to the island. The packed beaches seem a world away when you're out on the water. The crew on this incredible Catamaran are great fun to spend the day with. The atmposphere is friendly and relaxed and the resident DJ plays fantastic tunes to fit in with the vibe of the guests. and the speed of the boat. When we were out on the open water, the music was lively and exciting. When we were slowly meandering the coastline, or gliding alongside Es Vedra, the music was an eclectic mix of pop classics by the likes of Bowie and Yazoo. As we headed back towards Ibiza Town after sunset, the perfect sun-downer tunes were played.
