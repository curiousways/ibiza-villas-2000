As we are all scratching our heads wondering where did the summer go it's time for closing party fever to finally hit the island. After 2 years of seriously restricted partying, this year's offerings promise to be some of the best yet. Check out our pick of the bunch and the best areas to stay to squeeze every last bit of fun out of your stay.

Ushuaia and Hï

One More DANTS with Ants and Hi closing Sat 08 Oct 12:00 till late

Closing lineups really don’t get any bigger than this, Black Coffee fronts a giant program including The Martinez Brothers, Camelphat back-to-back Andrea Oliva, Cloonee, Latmun, Francisco Allendes, Chelina Manuhutu, Syreeta and Sona. We recommend buying a ticket for this one as it is selling out fast. Get your tickets here

Head over the road afterwards for the closing of superclub Hi, featuring Ibiza giants Tale Of Us and The Martinez Brothers joined by Club Room hosts Damian Lazarus and Vintage Culture. Cultural icon Peggy Gou completes the bill in the main room. Get your tickets here and don’t forget to buy the double ticket for both venues. Get your tickets here

Where to stay?

Villa Daniel is just a stone's throw from Playa de Bossa and sleeping up to 12 people it is the perfect spot for larger groups. Villa Daniel offers wonderful outdoor space with a large pool, sun loungers and shaded bamboo chill-out daybeds. If you would like to book Villa Daniel get in touch with our team here

Amnesia

Official closing party with Chemical Brothers Sat 15 Oct 18:00 - 06:00

Kicking off at 18:00 and running, until 06:00 the following day, this 12-hour spectacular is a true island favourite. With the mighty Chemical Brothers headlining it is set to sell out very quickly. We advise pacing yourself, peaking too soon might mean you miss the final moments of magic. Get your tickets here

Where to stay?

Can Tegui is just a short cab journey from Amnesia and offers a rustic, peaceful setting that is the perfect place to escape the closing party madness. To book Can Tegui for your group get in touch with our team here

Ibiza Rocks

Applebum closing Sat 01 Oct 14:00 - 21:00

Expect new school sounds with an old school attitude for sunkissed afternoons by the iconic candy pink Ibiza Rocks’ pool. With Khadejia DJ Policy and Danny Beck bringing the sounds, it promises to be an epic day, hedonism at its best. Get your tickets here

Where to stay?

Marian Aparthotel is a very short walk to Ibiza Rocks which means no expensive cabs or waiting for party buses. With all the home comforts that you would expect from one of our villas, these 1 and 2 bedroomed apartments are the best way to hit all of the big San An Closings this year. To book your stay get in touch with the team 

DC10

Circoloco Closing party Mon 10 Oct 16:00 - 07:00

Circoloco is possibly the biggest, most crazy party to be had on the island and its closing is no expectation, if you are only going to hit one closing in 2022 then this is the one that you can’t afford to miss. With a truly monster lineup that includes Damian Lazarus, Dixon, Honey Dijon, Joseph Capriati, Maceo Plex, Patrick Topping and Seth Troxler it is the closing party that closes the island. Tickets are absolutely essential and sell out fast, get yours here

Where to stay?

Casa Roig is located close to many of the beautiful beach bars on the southern tip of the island, Blue Marlin is just 10 minutes away and DC10 is just a short cab ride. Sleeping 6 people, this is the perfect villa for smaller groups and offers peaceful countryside views and an easily accessible location. To book Casa Roig get in touch with our team here
