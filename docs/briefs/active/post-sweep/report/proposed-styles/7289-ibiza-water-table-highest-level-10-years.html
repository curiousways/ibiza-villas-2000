The Ibiza water table measured in the underground aquifers for the month of January reached its highest level for 10 years. All thanks to the higher than average rainfall this winter which we reported on earlier this week.

Stable and normal

The Ibiza water table, having languished at a dangerously low level over the past couple of years is now considered to be stable and at normal levels. It may even rise more as it takes a little while for the rains to sink below the earth into the underground aquifers.

Ibiza gains most

Although most of the Balearics have seen an increase in the water table, Ibiza has benefited the most by far from the recent spate of stormy weather. The Ibiza water levels increased from 56% during December to a whopping 76% for the month of January, a respective increase of over 30% in one month! Just check out the rainfall graph below:

The islanders are cheering, make no mistake! Speak to pretty much anyone who lives in Ibiza all year round and they'll tell you that the winter rains are a blessing for the earth, so parched from the hot, dry Ibiza summer. Our whole lives revolve around water, which we often don't remember until there's a shortage of it.

Wine crisis!

Last year, for instance, there was a shortage of boutique wines in the San Mateo region of Ibiza due to the lack of rain. This was serious! It certainly got the attention of yours truly *hic* ;)
