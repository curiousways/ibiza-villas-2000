Where to Eat & Drink in San Antonio, Ibiza Without Breaking the Bank

San Antonio, Ibiza, is famous for its stunning sunsets, buzzing nightlife, and laid-back beach vibes. But if you're exploring Ibiza on a budget, you don't have to sacrifice great food. This guide highlights some of the best cheap restaurants in San Antonio, where you can enjoy delicious meals at affordable prices. Whether you're fueling up before the beach, recovering after a night out, or looking for the best places to drink in San Antonio, these spots offer incredible food and drinks without the hefty price tag.

Rita's Cantina

Nestled by the picturesque marina, Rita's Cantina is a go-to for both locals and visitors. Open from early morning until late at night, it serves a diverse menu catering to all tastes. Start your day with their famous club sandwich or the Spanish staple pan con tomate while soaking up the harbor views. Rita’s Cantina is one of the best choices for those visiting Ibiza on a budget, offering generous portions at reasonable prices.

Café Bondi

Situated on the bustling main promenade, Café Bondi is perfect for health-conscious diners—or those in need of a solid recovery meal after a big night out. The menu features a mix of nutritious and indulgent options, from fresh smoothie bowls to hearty full English breakfasts. If you're looking for cheap restaurants in San Antonio that also serve healthy options, Café Bondi is a great pick. Its prime location and laid-back atmosphere make it ideal for people-watching while enjoying great food.

Villa Manchega

For those craving authentic Spanish cuisine, Villa Manchega is a hidden gem on the outskirts of town. Known for its generous sharing platters, this family-run restaurant offers a mix of meats, seafood, and classic Spanish sides. The portions are hearty, the flavors are bold, and the prices are fair—making it a top choice for groups wanting local dishes without a big bill. It's a fantastic stop for anyone exploring Ibiza on a budget.

Palapa

Perched on the seafront near the famous Egg roundabout, Palapa delivers a bold Mexican menu with a side of stunning sea views. The menu is packed with vibrant tacos, loaded nachos, and refreshing cocktails—perfect for fueling up before a big night out. For those seeking the best places to drink in San Antonio, Palapa’s lively atmosphere and affordable prices make it a must-visit.

Rincon de Pepe

Tucked away in San Antonio’s old quarter, Rincon de Pepe is a charming spot for traditional Spanish tapas. The rustic setting adds to the experience as you work your way through a menu of classic small plates—think patatas bravas, grilled chorizo, and jamón ibérico. It's one of the best cheap restaurants in San Antonio, offering high-quality food at budget-friendly prices.

Pizzeria David's

If you’re craving pizza, Pizzeria David’s is the place to go. A staple in San Antonio’s restaurant scene, this popular eatery serves up crisp, Neapolitan-style pizzas loaded with fresh toppings. Located just a short walk from the Sunset Strip, it’s the perfect spot for a post-sunset carb fix before heading out for the night. Friendly service, great prices, and consistently good food make it a local and tourist favorite.

Kumaras – Breathtaking San Antonio Bay Views

Perched on the seafront in San Antonio Bay, Kumaras offers a unique blend of global street food, refreshing cocktails, and one of the best sunset views on the island. The venue features a chill-out area, lounge, and street food market, capturing the essence of Ibiza with effortless boho style. If you're looking for the best places to drink in San Antonio, Kumaras is the perfect spot to unwind with a cocktail while watching the sunset—all without breaking the bank.

Final Thoughts

Exploring Ibiza on a budget doesn’t mean compromising on food and drinks. From cozy tapas bars to lively beachside eateries, these cheap restaurants in San Antonio offer unbeatable value. And if you're looking for the best places to drink in San Antonio, don’t miss spots like Palapa and Kumaras for sunset cocktails at budget-friendly prices. Want to stay close to all the action? Check out Villa Tunicu, Villa Nieves, and Villa Can Vincent, or book a stay at Aparthotel Marian for easy access to San Antonio’s top dining and nightlife spots.
