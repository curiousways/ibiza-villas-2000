Things to do in the winter in Ibiza: Part 1

Although Ibiza is mostly recognized as a summer destination, it may come as a surprise that winter in Ibiza does exist, and it can even get a little chilly at times. Winter in Ibiza is a perfect time to enjoy the fabulous weather, the nature and to visit the island. Once the summer tourists have left, the winter social calendar starts and new spots can be discovered on the island. During this period, the sunsets are exceptional. You will discover impressive red/orange scenes. Whether it's nature, enjoying with the family, or finding a sporting activity, the off-season will offer you a multitude of ways to keep you busy.

Weather in Ibiza

You can expect temperatures from 3℃ to around 15℃ and many people still very bravely take to the seas for a swim during the winter months. Many of the chiringuitos remain open including The Jockey club and Yemanja. And what better way to round off a winter swim or walk than with some well-deserved liquid refreshment?

Winter walk

With almost an entire year in the sun, the possibilities of getting out into nature in Ibiza are not limited to the summer. Winter walking in Ibiza will allow you to enjoy the Ibiza landscape while expanding your social life. Weekly guided walks are an opportunity to make new friends, build a network, and discover hidden corners of the island. There are organized walks on the island that will take you to hidden coves and secret beaches. You can also explore the lesser-known inside of the island, where you'll find such diverse points of reference as ancient waterways, endless fields, wetlands with wildlife, and plenty of pine forests. Almond Blossom

In February, the Ibizan countryside is in full bloom, with its lush vegetation, abundant wildlife, and stunning views. The rolling hills and valleys, as well as the bright blue sky, create a magical atmosphere that can be enjoyed to the fullest. Take a day trip to explore the beauty of Ibiza's rural landscapes, and take in its many wonders. Whether you choose to go hiking or cycling, you will be surrounded by stunning landscapes, with breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea. You can also visit some of the villages of the island, where you can sample traditional food and meet friendly locals. Exploring Ibiza's almond blossoms is a magical experience. From hiking and cycling to photography, these trees provide the perfect backdrop for a variety of outdoor activities. As you take in the stunning views, you can breathe in the scent of pine, thyme, and juniper, adding to the mystical atmosphere. On a full moon night in Santa Inés, the trees are transformed into an enchanting landscape of glimmering silver. Sunsets

The cold weather doesn't have to stop you from enjoying the beauty of Ibiza's sunsets. Hostel la Torre, situated in Cap Negret just outside of San Antonio, is the perfect place to view the stunning display of colors in the sky. Due to its popularity, it is important to book ahead to ensure you get a spot at this great location. Once you are there, you can forget your worries and enjoy the spellbinding atmosphere. If you really want to avoid the crowds then head to one of the beaches on the Southwest coast, depending on the time of year, Cala Tarida, Cala Vedella and Es Cubells all offer stunning sunset locations without the usual summer hordes.

Markets

Head to Ibiza’s winter markets for an awesome chance to snag some steals and catch up with your pals! SAN JORDI Flea Market For more than two and a half decades, San Jordi Market has been an Ibiza staple. From second-hand clothing to jewelry, leather goods and household items, this is the place to be on a Saturday morning. Located at the Hippodrome near San Jordi village, both locals and tourists come to browse the unique selection of goods. Get there early to find the best deals! LAS DALIAS San Carlos The Las Dalias Hippy Market in San Carlos, in the north of the island, draws in visitors every weekend during the winter season. It promises a plethora of handmade art and craft for sale, accompanied by music events and the famous stone baked pizza served up in the garden. Make sure to stop by and enjoy a few hours of shopping and entertainment at this unique destination. Head to Las Dalias this winter for the ultimate Ibiza weekender experience! On the weekends, Hippie Markets offer unique trinkets and food, while DJs spin their tunes during the day. As the night falls, the club AKASHA is the place to be, hosting some of the best DJs in the island. Enjoying the distinct atmosphere of Las Dalias and the unbeatable entertainment is a must-do during the winter season in Ibiza.
