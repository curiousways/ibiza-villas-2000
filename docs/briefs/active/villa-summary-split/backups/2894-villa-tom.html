Villa Tom (Can Petit) is a comfortable, modern villa in Sa Carroca, a five-minute drive from Playa d'en Bossa and within easy reach of Ibiza Town — with a bar, supermarket and bus stop all a five-minute walk away. One of our most consistently popular houses, it has four bedrooms and four bathrooms, and suits groups and families who want a hassle-free base; its first-floor terrace looks across to Dalt Vila and, on a clear day, Formentera.

Inside the villa

Set back from the road behind a large gate with ample parking, the front door opens onto a comfortable living and dining area on one side and a well-equipped kitchen on the other, with a door straight out to the pool.

  • Ground floor — a double with its own en-suite shower room, and a twin with a separate bathroom
  • First floor — a twin, and the master: a magnificent four-poster bed, a huge walk-in wardrobe, a shower room and a wonderfully quirky circular Jacuzzi bath set by the window overlooking the pool

The pool and outside space

The garden backs onto open fields — a lovely large pool, a shaded terrace with dining table, and a Balinese-style canopied seating area for when you want out of the sun.
