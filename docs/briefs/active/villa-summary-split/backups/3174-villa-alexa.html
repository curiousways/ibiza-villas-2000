Villa Alexa (Can Vincent) is a simple, peaceful country villa a ten-minute walk up a country camino from the heart of San José village — sitting almost exactly between Playa d'en Bossa and San Antonio, with the beautiful west-coast beaches close by. The property is two buildings arranged around a fantastic shaded terrace, so groups and families can spread out and then come together in the outdoor spaces — three air-conditioned bedrooms sleeping six, and a great base for a party who all want different things from their holiday.

Inside the villa

A long private driveway leads to the original farmhouse, where the dining room opens through double doors onto the shaded terrace between the two buildings. The bedrooms — including a studio double off the dining room — are split between the farmhouse and the annex, with independent modern shower rooms in each building; upstairs in the annex there's a second kitchen and dining area with its own shower room.

The pool and outside space

The 10-metre by 5-metre pool sits in lovely Mediterranean gardens full of fruit trees and flowers, with stunning open views across the countryside.

Good to know

San José itself has very good bars, cafés and restaurants — some with live Spanish music at night — plus pretty shops and occasional night markets in the church square.
