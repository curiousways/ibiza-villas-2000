Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. This page lists the cookies this site sets, what they do, and how to change your choice.

## Your choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are set either way, because the site does not work without them. You can change your answer at any time using the Cookie preferences link in the footer. Withdrawing consent removes the analytics cookies from your device and stops new ones being set. It does not affect anything recorded before you withdrew.

## Strictly necessary cookies

These are set by this website and do not require consent.

| Cookie | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| `cc_cookie` | Remembers the choice you made in the cookie banner so we do not ask again | 6 months |

## Analytics cookies

Set only if you accept them. We use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited and how people move through the site, so we can improve it. The data is aggregated and we do not use it to identify you. Google's own privacy policy applies to how Google handles this data.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes one visitor from another | 2 years |
| `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Keeps the session state | 2 years |

## Cookies we do not set

This site does not use advertising cookies, and it does not carry social media plugins or embedded content from third parties that set their own cookies. Links to our Facebook and Instagram pages are ordinary links.

## Forms and enquiries

When you send an enquiry or request to book, the details you enter are sent to us by email and stored in the site's form records so we can reply. That is covered in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/), not by cookies.
